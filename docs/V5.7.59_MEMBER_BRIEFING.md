# Virtual NATO Flight Planning Suite — V5.7.59 Member Briefing

Release date: 15 September 2026  
Channel: TEAM LIVE

## What changed

- The left panel now scrolls below the theme image so the selected artwork, UTC/local clocks and theme control remain visible.
- The right panel now scrolls below the suite title and navigation/tool buttons so those controls remain available while reading or working lower on the page.
- Mobile devices retain the normal single-page scrolling layout.
- The DAY theme artwork now uses a corrected C-17 Globemaster III with four matching engines, symmetrical wings and nacelles, a correct T-tail and retracted landing gear.

## Member action

Refresh the LIVE page once before use. If the previous layout or DAY image remains cached, use a hard refresh (`Ctrl+F5`).

## Preserved behaviour

Flight-planning, SimBrief, SWIFT, VATSIM, Oceanic and sortie-profile workflows are unchanged. DUSK, NIGHT and NIGHT OPS artwork is unchanged. The Operational Disclaimer remains in force.

The full member manual for the current workflows remains **V5.7.58 REV10**.
