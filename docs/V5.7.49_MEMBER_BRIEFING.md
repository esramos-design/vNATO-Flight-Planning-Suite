# vNATO Flight Planning Suite — Member Briefing V5.7.49

Release date: 12 SEP 2026  
Audience: All Virtual NATO members  
Status: TEAM LIVE

## What changed

V5.7.49 improves filing of mixed IFR/VFR military flight plans through myVATSIM.

The Suite now provides two flight-plan copies when an ICAO `Y` or `Z` plan needs myVATSIM compatibility:

1. **ICAO copy** — retains the correct ICAO `Y` or `Z` flight-rule designator.
2. **VATSIM version** — uses the initial IFR or VFR rule accepted by the myVATSIM form and places the original mixed-rule information and transition points in RMK.

Buttons are available for both the current and BETA myVATSIM flight-plan pages. The two output boxes use the same layout, with each Copy button on the right.

## Which copy should I use?

- Use the **ICAO copy** when the receiving system supports ICAO `Y` and `Z`.
- Use **Copy VATSIM version** when importing into a myVATSIM form that offers only IFR and VFR.
- The VATSIM version does not remove the mixed-rule plan: the original ICAO rule and transition points are carried in RMK for review and coordination.

## Before filing

After import, always confirm:

- the initial Flight Rules selection;
- every IFR/VFR or GAT/OAT transition in the route;
- RMK, including the mixed-rule explanation and OAT status;
- departure, destination, date, time, speed, altitude and endurance;
- equipment, PBN, NAV, STS, SEL, PER, registration and operator values;
- all STAY blocks and their associated STAYINFO descriptions.

The Suite assists planning and formatting. It does not replace current vNATO procedures, national requirements, LOAs, VATSIM rules, ATC coordination or pilot responsibility.
